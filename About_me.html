About Me

Who is Robin Rigiani
Also known as Robba Locka

Robin Rigiani, professionally known as Robba Locka, is a multidisciplinary creative, performer, and entrepreneur, and the founder of Robba Locka Entertainment. With a strong foundation in performance, production, and creative direction, Robin has built a career at the intersection of freestyle, entertainment, culture, and choreography.

Robin graduated from Rijn IJssel in 2017, where he began developing his professional skillset and creative discipline. He continued his artistic education at the Amsterdam University of the Arts (Amsterdamse Hogeschool voor de Kunsten), graduating in 2022, further refining his craft and deepening his understanding of the creative and performing arts industry.

In 2020, Robin achieved a major competitive milestone by winning Juste Debout Rotterdam, one of the most respected international street dance competitions. This accomplishment solidified his reputation as a high-level performer and creative force within the dance and urban arts community.

Beyond performance, Robin has successfully translated his artistic vision into the commercial space. Through The Movers Amsterdam, he has worked with major brands, including Philips, contributing creative talent, production support, and performance-driven storytelling to professional campaigns and projects.

Today, Robin continues to bridge talent development, production, and brand experience, focusing on creating impactful entertainment experiences that elevate both artists and clients. His work is defined by authenticity, creative excellence, and a commitment to bringing talent into the spotlight.
